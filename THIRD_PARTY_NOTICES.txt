UIFramework by Starficz (from "Refit Filters")
Licensed under LGPL-3.0-only: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html

Vendored into five modules, each under its own package:
    stopbloatingme.uiframework, stopstackingme.uiframework, intelrenewed.uiframework,
    hullmodsrenewed.uiframework, starterpack.uiframework
Unmodified apart from the package rename and the visibility changes needed to use it from
outside its own package. Source is in src/<module>/uiframework in the repository above, and
every file there keeps its original licence header.


Gambling Den: Interastral Peace Casino
Authors: Emanon6 and WolframSegler
Reference version: 1.6.3

The blackjack rules model in src/gamblingden/blackjack/BlackjackGame.java is
adapted from Interastral Peace Casino's BlackjackGame.java and Deck.java.
Its original notice in Licences.txt, under "For the rest of the mod:", is:

Free to use, modify and redistribute for non commercial purposes.

This permission applies to the adapted code. Changes include token accounting,
bet validation, dealer-blackjack precedence, corrected split-hand settlement,
split-ace rules, exact integer payouts, incremental dealer play, and safe exits.

The full-card sprite drawing in CardArt.java follows Interastral's
CardSprites.java and CardRenderingUtils.java. It adds private asset paths,
preloading, alpha handling, and a missing-art guard. Its adapted code uses
the same non-commercial permission above.


Gambling Den: SVG-cards artwork
Original creator: David Bellot
Fork and improvements: Huub de Beer (htdebeer) and contributors
Project: https://github.com/htdebeer/SVG-cards
Upstream revision: 6d88bf9c594997b82bc5668ab0877a4e9b717665
License: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1

graphics/gamblingden/cards/ contains 52 unmodified faces and the red back
from the upstream png/2x exports of the deck used by Interastral Peace Casino.
These artwork files retain their LGPL terms, independently of the code's
non-commercial terms above. No additional restrictions apply to the artwork.

licenses/svg-cards/ includes the original LICENSE, AUTHORS, README.md,
svg-cards.svg, and editable svg-cards-indented.svg. The upstream README
documents rendering and links its PNG export tools. The PNGs and source
are unchanged. You can replace the loose card images without rebuilding
the mod. No casino music, logos, or table artwork is included.
