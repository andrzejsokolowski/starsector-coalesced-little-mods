WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
- Puts eight of my small mods into one download. Each one has its own on/off switch.


One mod in the list instead of eight. Don't want one of them? Switch it off in LunaLib's settings and it is gone after a restart.

[spoiler=FAQ]
[b]I already use some of these separately. What do I do?[/b]
Disable the separate versions, then enable the pack. The game refuses to start if both are on, and tells you which ones to disable. Your saves keep working, and the pack copies your settings from the separate mods the first time it starts.
[b]What happens when I switch a module off?[/b]
It stops. Its buttons, dialogue options and menus disappear, and its buildings can no longer be built. Buildings you already have keep working, and nothing is removed from your save. Switch it back on and everything is back.
[b]Does the pack change the separate mods' settings?[/b]
No. It only reads them once, so they are still there if you go back.
